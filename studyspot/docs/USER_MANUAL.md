# StudySpot - User Manual

StudySpot lets you find and book a study room on campus. This manual has two parts: one for **students and staff**, one for **administrators**.

---

## Part A - Students and staff

### A1. Create an account and log in
1. Open StudySpot and click **Create account**.
2. Enter your full name, student/staff number, email, phone (optional) and a password (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number). Press **Register**.
3. Go back to **Log in**, enter your email and password. After logging in you see the room search page.
4. To leave, click **Logout** at the top right.

**Forgot your password?** Click *Forgot password?* on the login page, enter your email and open the reset link. The link works once and expires after 30 minutes.

### A2. Find a room
1. Click **Rooms**.
2. Choose the **date** and the **From / To** times. Optionally set a minimum capacity, an equipment item (for example *Projector*) or type a room/building name.
3. Click **Search**. Each room shows a badge: **Available**, **Booked** (taken at that time) or **Maintenance**.

### A3. Book a room
1. On an available room click **Book now**.
2. Check the date and times. The coloured strip shows the day: green = free, red = taken, blue = your selection.
3. Enter the number of attendees and, if you wish, the purpose. Click **Confirm booking**.
4. You see a confirmation message and the booking appears in **My Bookings**. A notification is added under the bell icon.

Rules: rooms are open 07:00-21:00; times are on 30-minute marks; a booking lasts 30 minutes to 4 hours; you can book up to 14 days ahead;
you can hold at most 5 upcoming bookings and cannot be in two rooms at the same time.
Bookings longer than 2 hours are **Pending** until an administrator approves them.

### A4. Change or cancel a booking
Open **My Bookings** -> **Upcoming**. Click **Edit** to change the time or number of people, or **Cancel** to release the room (you will be asked to confirm).
The **Past** and **Cancelled** tabs show your history. A booking that has already started cannot be changed.

### A5. Profile
Under **Profile** you can change your name and phone number and set a new password.

### A6. If something goes wrong
| Message | What it means / what to do |
|---------|-----------------------------|
| "Incorrect email or password" | Check spelling; use *Forgot password?* if needed. |
| "...already booked for part of the time you chose" | Someone booked it first. Choose another time or room. |
| "You already have another booking at that time" | You can only be in one room at a time. |
| "Your account has been suspended" | Contact the administrator. |
| "Cannot reach the server" | The StudySpot server is not running or you are offline. |

## Part B - Administrators

Log in with an administrator account; you are taken to the **Admin Panel**. Use the left menu.

### B1. Dashboard
Shows the number of rooms, today's bookings, active users, recent cancellations, a bar chart of the last 7 days and the latest bookings.
A yellow box appears when bookings wait for your approval - click **Review now**.

### B2. Rooms
- **Add new room:** fill in name, building, floor, capacity, status and tick the equipment (with quantity). Click **Save room**.
- **Edit / Delete:** use the buttons in the table. A room with booking history cannot be deleted - set its status to *Maintenance* instead.
- **Search:** type a name or building and press the search button.
- **Equipment catalogue:** add new equipment items or remove unused ones at the bottom of the page.

### B3. Users
- **Register user:** enter the details; leave the password empty to create a temporary one, which is shown once so you can give it to the user.
- **Edit, suspend/activate, reset password, delete** with the buttons in each row. Users with bookings cannot be deleted - suspend them.
- **Filters:** search by name/email/ID, or filter by role and status.
- Only a **Super Administrator** can create or change administrator accounts.

### B4. Bookings
Filter by date, room, status or user; click a column heading to sort. **Approve** pending bookings, **Cancel** a booking (you may give a reason; the user is notified) or view details with the eye button.

### B5. Reports
1. Choose the report type: **Room usage** or **Bookings list**.
2. Set the period, room (and status for the bookings list), and how to sort.
3. Click **Generate report**. The report shows the university name, logo, date generated and the filters used.
4. Click **Print / Save as PDF** (choose "Save as PDF" as the printer) or **Export CSV** for Excel.

### B6. Audit logs
A record of important actions (logins, bookings, changes) with the user, time and IP address. Search by action, user or date.
